THE OPEN-DAY PACKING CHECKLIST

A starting point for a casual day out. Adapt it to the weather, route,
duration, and your own needs. Remote or technical trips need more planning.

[ ] A tee you feel good in
[ ] Comfortable shoes for your route
[ ] A layer for changing weather
[ ] Water and a snack
[ ] Sun protection
[ ] A charged phone and an offline map
[ ] Keys, ID, and a way to pay
[ ] Any personal essentials or medication
[ ] A small bag for your rubbish

Before you go: check the forecast, know your way back, and tell someone
where you're headed if you'll be exploring alone.

Leave places as you found them. Leave a little room for the unexpected.

Wear less. Wander more.
